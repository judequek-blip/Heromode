Make tracking work

Face the camera in good lighting. Hold the Superman, Spider-Man, and Iron Man poses for at least a quarter of a second. Their effects repeat while held; lower your arm to stop. If a pose is not recognized, try the corresponding button.

This app detects body positions, not finger signs, open palms, fists, or eye movements. Masks and the Iron Man faceplate use buttons. Strength, agility, spider-sense, and god mode are descriptive labels without separate controls.

Superman flight replaces the background with a moving sky behind your silhouette for four seconds. Thor summons a hammer for four seconds; it follows your right hand and also appears during lightning. Hold their poses to repeat.

Spider-Man: either arm shoots webs, a continuous two-second hold starts city swinging, and both arms cover the screen in webbing. Lower both arms to stop. Losing tracking resets the hold timer. These detect arm poses, not finger signs.

Photos and privacy

Download saves a PNG of your camera view with effects. Back releases the camera. Frames are processed in your browser and are not uploaded by the app. Internet access is needed to download the tracking model from jsDelivr. Camera access requires HTTPS or localhost.

Need help?

Allow camera permission and close other apps using your webcam. If tracking fails to load, check your connection. Keep joints visible and try the power buttons to distinguish gesture recognition from rendering.
